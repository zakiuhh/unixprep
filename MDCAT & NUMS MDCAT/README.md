<p style="font-size: 20px; text-align: center">🩺 MDCAT & NUMS MDCAT</p>

Prep resources for the Medical & Dental College Admission Test (MDCAT), plus NUMS MDCAT — the separate test conducted by the National University of Medical Sciences for admission to Army Medical College and affiliated institutions.

## 📋 About the Tests

- **MDCAT** (provincial/PMDC-regulated): covers Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English, and Logical Reasoning, based on FSc Pre-Medical syllabus.
- **NUMS MDCAT**: separate exam, similar subject spread, but with its own paper pattern and past papers — don't assume it's identical to standard MDCAT.

Always check the current year's official syllabus split, since MDCAT weightage and format have changed across recent years.

## 📝 How to Use

1. Biology usually carries the most weight — prioritize accordingly, but don't neglect Chemistry.
2. Practice past papers under timed conditions; both tests are heavily MCQ- and speed-based.
3. Keep a running log of recurring topics/mistakes rather than just redoing full papers blindly.
