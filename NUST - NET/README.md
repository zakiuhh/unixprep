<p style="font-size: 20px; text-align: center">🚀 NUST - NET — NUST Entry Test</p>
Prep resources for the NUST Entry Test (NET), used for admission to NUST's engineering, CS, and other undergraduate programs.

## 📋 About the Test

NET generally covers Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, and Intelligence/IQ reasoning, matching the subject folders below. It's known for being conceptually tough and time-pressured, so past paper practice and pacing matter a lot here.

- **Subjects** — subject-wise notes and practice content, the best starting point.
- **NUST Past Question Collection / Challange MCQs / NET PakPrep Mcqs / Random NUST MCQs** — community/self-compiled MCQ sets, generally fine to keep as original or freely-shared content.
- **KIPS - FUNG - STEP** and **KIPS NET FLPs** — ⚠️ these reference paid coaching academies (KIPS, FUNG, STEP). If this material came from their paid courses/books, replace the folder with a link to the academy instead of hosting the files — see the root README's copyright note.

## 📝 How to Use

1. Start with **Subjects** to build concepts.
2. Move to MCQ banks for topic-wise drilling.
3. Use full-length paper collections for timed mocks once you're comfortable subject-by-subject.

## 🤝 Contributing

Original notes, self-made MCQs, and freely-shared past questions are welcome. Please don't upload scanned copies of paid academy books — link to them instead.
