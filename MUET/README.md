<p style="font-size: 20px; text-align: center">⚙️ MUET — Mehran University Engineering Test</p>

Prep resources for the MUET entry test, used for engineering admissions at Mehran University of Engineering & Technology, Jamshoro.

## 📋 About the Test

MUET generally assesses FSc Pre-Engineering level concepts across Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English. Format and weightage can shift year to year, so treat older past papers as practice material, not a guaranteed blueprint of the current test.

## 🗂️ Contents

This folder is currently a flat collection — consider organizing it as it grows:


## 📝 How to Use

1. Attempt full-length past papers under timed conditions to build exam stamina.
2. Track weak subjects/topics after each attempt instead of just moving to the next paper.
3. Pair past papers with FSc textbook revision — MUET questions usually stay close to the board syllabus.
