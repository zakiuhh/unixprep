How to Contribute

Got notes, past papers, or MCQs sitting on your laptop? Here's how to add them to the repo so other students can use them too.

Adding material, step by step

01

Fork the repository — click "Fork" in the top-right of the GitHub page to make your own copy.

02

Add your files — inside your fork, open the folder for the relevant test (or create a new one if it doesn't exist yet), and upload your files there.

03

Keep it organized — name files clearly (e.g. Physics-Chapter-3-Notes.pdf) and place them under the right subject folder.

04

Commit your changes — GitHub will ask for a short commit message describing what you added.

05

Open a Pull Request — from your fork, click "Contribute" → "Open pull request" so we can review it.

06

Wait for review — we'll take a look and merge it in, or leave a comment if something needs a small tweak first.

A few guidelines:

  • Prefer PDF for documents so formatting stays consistent.
  • Don't upload copyrighted or paywalled material.
  • One test, one folder — don't mix subjects together.
  • Keep files free of ads or watermarks where possible.
