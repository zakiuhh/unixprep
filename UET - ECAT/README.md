<p style="font-size: 20px; text-align: center">🔧 UET - ECAT — Engineering College Admission Test</p>

Prep resources for ECAT, the standardized test used for engineering admissions across Punjab's public universities (UET and affiliated colleges).

## 📋 About the Test

ECAT covers Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry based on the FSc Pre-Engineering syllabus, with a heavy MCQ, time-pressured format. It's one of the most competitive entry tests in Punjab, so consistent past-paper practice matters more here than almost anywhere else.

## 📝 How to Use

1. Build concepts subject-by-subject using **ECAT Subjects**.
2. Move to **ECAT Past Papers** for full timed mocks, working backwards from the most recent year available.
3. Track recurring mistakes across attempts instead of just redoing papers passively.
