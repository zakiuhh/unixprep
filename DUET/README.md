<p style="font-size: 20px; text-align: center">🏛️ DUET — Dawood University of Engineering & Technology Entry Test</p>

Prep resources for the DUET entry test, used for engineering admissions at Dawood University of Engineering & Technology, Karachi.

## 📋 About the Test

DUET typically assesses FSc Pre-Engineering level concepts across Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (format and weightage can change year to year, so always cross-check the current pattern on DUET's official admissions page before relying on older papers for structure).

## 🗂️ Contents

- **PAST PAPERS** — Previous years' DUET entry test papers. Unorganize by year, if you wanna add more, just contribute as mentioned in Contributing section.

## 📝 How to Use

1. Start with the most recent past papers first — they're closest to the current format.
2. Time yourself when attempting a full paper to build exam-pace stamina.
3. Track which subject (Math/Physics/Chemistry) you're losing the most marks in and prioritize revision there.
4. Cross-check any answer keys against your textbook — past paper answer keys floating around online aren't always accurate.

## 🤝 Contributing

Have an older or more recent DUET past paper? Add it to `PAST PAPERS` with a clear filename including the year. Solutions/answer keys are welcome as a separate file.
